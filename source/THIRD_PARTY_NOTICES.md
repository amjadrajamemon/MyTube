MyTube uses the ML Kit on-device translation library (`com.google.mlkit:translate:17.0.3`) for the optional Live Dub feature. Translation models are downloaded on demand and processed on-device. Distribution remains subject to the applicable Google ML Kit / Android SDK terms.
